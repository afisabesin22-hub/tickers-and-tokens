GETTING STARTED

Financial Market Fundamentals

Understanding the foundations of investing can help you make sense of financial news and market developments.

LESSON 01

What Are Stocks?

Stocks represent ownership in a company. When investors purchase shares, they become shareholders and may benefit if the company's value increases.

However, share prices can also fall, meaning investors may lose money.

LESSON 02

What Is Cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency refers to digital assets that use technologies such as blockchain to record transactions and ownership.

Bitcoin and Ethereum are well-known examples. Their prices can fluctuate significantly over short periods.

LESSON 03

What Is Diversification?

Diversification involves spreading investments across different assets, industries or markets rather than relying on a single investment.

It can help manage investment risk, although it cannot eliminate the possibility of losses.

FINANCIAL TERMINOLOGY

Essential Market Terms

Financial news often includes technical terminology. Here are some common terms worth understanding.

Market Capitalisation
The total market value of a company's outstanding shares, calculated using its share price and share count.
Volatility
A measure of how much an asset's price fluctuates over time.
Bull Market
A period of generally rising prices and positive market sentiment.
Bear Market
A period of falling market prices, often accompanied by negative investor sentiment.
Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF)
An investment fund traded on an exchange. ETFs may hold shares, bonds or other assets.
Dividend
A payment that a company may distribute to its shareholders, often from company profits.

Further definitions: Investor.gov Financial Glossary

KNOW THE RISKS

Understanding Investment Risk

Investing involves uncertainty. The value of stocks, cryptocurrencies and other financial assets can rise or fall due to economic conditions, company performance or market sentiment.

Cryptocurrency investments may introduce additional risks, including substantial price volatility, security concerns, fraud and differences in investor protection.

Understanding investment products, researching reliable information and considering potential losses are important parts of financial education.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between stocks and cryptocurrency?

Stocks represent ownership interests in companies. Cryptocurrencies are digital assets whose characteristics and associated rights vary by asset.

Why do financial markets change in value?

Prices change as buyers and sellers respond to economic developments, company announcements, supply, demand and investor expectations.

Can investing involve financial losses?

Yes. Investments can lose value, and some can result in the loss of the entire amount invested.
