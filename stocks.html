How Does the Stock Market Work?

The stock market allows investors to buy and sell shares in publicly traded companies. Buying shares represents ownership of a small portion of a company.

Share prices can change based on company performance, economic developments, interest rates and investor expectations. Stock market indices help track the performance of selected groups of shares.
