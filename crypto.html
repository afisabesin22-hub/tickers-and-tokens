Understanding Cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrencies are digital assets that can be transferred or held using blockchain and other distributed ledger technologies. Bitcoin and Ethereum are two well-known examples.

Digital asset markets can experience significant price fluctuations and involve risks such as fraud, cyberattacks and loss of access to digital wallets.
